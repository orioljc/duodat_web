IMPORTANT! How should my uploaded images look like?

  • Make sure thre is only one person on each image

  • The face of the person should be clear and visible, and the person should be well recognizable

  • Both portraits and full body images are OK, but make sure the face is not too small

  • Pick the images you like the most since the AI output will be based on them

  • In general, more images (if good images) provides a better output, but different (number of) images will always produce different results

Where it hurts

Buyers call exactly when your team cannot pick up.

  • Enquiries come in at weekends and late in the evening, precisely when the office is empty.
  • Anyone who gets no answer calls the next agency: the lead is lost in minutes, not days.
  • Your team repeats the same square metres, prices and availability twenty times a day.
  • Viewings get booked with people who never said what they want or whether they have financing.
How Duodat solves it

An agent that knows your listings and knows what to ask.

It knows your listings

We load your properties with reference, price, size, bedrooms and area. The agent answers with real, up-to-date data and never promises something that does not exist.

Qualifies before the viewing

Budget, area, number of bedrooms, whether they need a mortgage and when they want to move. You arrive at the viewing knowing who you are talking to.

Books the viewing in your calendar

It offers the real free slots in your calendar and creates the appointment. If you would rather approve them yourself, it leaves the request pending confirmation.

Answers when you cannot

After hours, at weekends or when every line is busy. It can also pick up overflow calls only.

Sends the property details

After the call, the caller receives the property details by email along with the viewing confirmation and the address.

What is included

  • Initial load of your property listings
  • A qualification script tailored to your agency
  • Inbound call answering in three languages
  • Viewings created in Google Calendar or Outlook
  • A summary of each call and the lead details by email
  • Transfer to an agent when the call calls for it

What it does not do

  • It does not negotiate prices or terms — that stays with your sales team.
  • It does not sign anything and does not take payments or deposits over the phone.
  • It does not replace the in-person viewing or your team's advice.
Frequently asked questions

Questions about this use case

How do you keep the listings up to date?
We can read them from a spreadsheet, from a file your CRM exports, or from a direct connection if your software allows it. At minimum a daily refresh; with a live connection, in real time.
Can it handle several calls at once?
Yes. Unlike a person, the agent handles multiple calls in parallel, so nobody gets an engaged tone during a campaign or a busy weekend.
What if the caller wants to speak to a human?
The agent transfers the call to the number you give us during office hours, or takes their details and the best time to call back if nobody is available.
Does it work for lettings too?
Yes. The qualification criteria change (payslips, guarantors, move-in date) and the script adapts. If you work both markets, the agent asks what the caller is after first.

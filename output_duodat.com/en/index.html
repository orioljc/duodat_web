The problem

The phone is the bottleneck in your day

Every call that goes unanswered — or never gets made — has a cost: a missed appointment, a worried family, a customer who goes to your competitor.

Calls are lost after hours

Someone who calls at 9pm and gets no answer does not call back: they call the next business on the list.

Repetitive tasks burn out your team

Confirming appointments, reminding people about visits and following up takes hours of qualified staff time every week.

Daily care cannot be delegated

Calling a dependent relative every single day is hard to keep up, and not calling leaves you anxious.

Frequently asked questions

What everyone asks us

Do people realise they are talking to a machine?
Usually yes, and we do not hide it: the agent introduces itself as an automated assistant at the start of the call. The goal is not to fool anyone, but to make the conversation clear, polite and useful. That is why the agent always says who it is calling on behalf of: knowing that completely changes how the call lands.
What happens if the agent does not understand something?
First it asks again, phrased differently. If it still does not understand, or if the person asks for a human, it transfers the call to a number you give us or raises an alert to call back. The agent does not make up answers.
Which languages does it speak?
Catalan, Spanish and English. It detects which language it is being spoken to in and replies in that language. If you need another one, tell us and we will look into it.
Do I have to change my phone or my operator?
No. You can keep your number and forward calls to the agent when you close, or give it a new number for outbound calls. There is no equipment to buy.
How quickly can it be up and running?
For standard cases, within a few days of our first conversation. Integrations with management software can take longer, and we will tell you that before we start.
Who can listen to the calls?
Only you and the people you authorise. The Duodat team only accesses them to resolve incidents, under a confidentiality agreement. You can switch off audio recording and keep only the summary.
Is it suitable for emergencies?
No. Duodat is not an emergency service or a medical alert service. If the agent picks up on something worrying, it immediately notifies the contacts you have configured so they can act — but in an emergency you must call the emergency number (112 in Spain and across the EU).
How much does it cost?
From €19 per month for families and from €99 per month for businesses. The final quote depends on call volume and on the integrations you need, and we give it to you in writing before anything starts.
